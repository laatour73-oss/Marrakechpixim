The Marrakechpixim operating method

The Three-Part Method — From Discovery to Cooperation

Marrakechpixim operates as a discovery, advertising and business-intermediation platform. It helps people identify relevant options and, where authorised, creates a traceable introduction between an interested customer and a business.

01

Discovery & intelligent search

The customer searches by product, service, company, sector or destination. MPX assistance can help organise the request, compare public information and support multilingual communication. Listings are discovery information, not a guarantee or endorsement.

02

Introduction & connection

With the customer’s request and the relevant company’s permission, Marrakechpixim can forward a qualified enquiry or referral. A unique reference should connect the customer request, selected sector and receiving company.

03

Agreement & follow-up

The customer and company discuss availability, price and conditions directly or through an authorised intermediary. Any commission, service fee or referral payment must be disclosed and accepted in a written agreement before it is due. The request can then be tracked by its reference and status.

Important: A search or introduction does not itself create a sale, partnership, payment obligation or confirmed booking. Marrakechpixim does not claim to represent a company without written authorisation.
