Technology and transaction roadmap

Separate product codes, service codes and payment codes.

The target catalogue is planned to grow towards 1,700+ catalogue entries across the platform. This is a development target, not a claim that 1,700 live products, companies or verified listings are already connected.

â–¦

1. Product or service code

Each product or service should receive its own unique internal reference so the platform can identify its category, source and record. A product/service code is not proof of payment.

Proposed examples: MP-HTL (hotels), MP-AIR (air travel), MP-JWY (jewellery), MP-AUT (cars), MP-MOT (motorcycles), MP-MED (medical equipment), MP-SPT (sports equipment), MP-CBL (cables), MP-MFG (manufacturing), MP-DIG (digital services).

â–¤

2. Transaction reference

Every order or transaction should have a separate, unique reference. The system must record the relevant product/service, parties, currency, amount, status and timestamps, with controls against duplicate records and manipulation.

Example format only: MP-TXN-YYYYMMDD-UNIQUEID. The production format must be generated and checked by the backend.

â–£

3. Payment QR code

A payment QR code must be separate from the product code and transaction reference. It should be generated or confirmed by the selected payment provider when that provider is integrated. Scanning a QR code alone does not prove payment.

Required control: verify payment status with the provider or trusted backend before confirming payment, commission or referral credit.

â–¥

4. Official retail barcode

Where a manufacturer or seller uses an official GS1/GTIN barcode, preserve it as a separate external identifier. Marrakechpixim's internal reference must not be presented as an official GS1/GTIN barcode unless it has been properly registered.

Data discipline: keep internal IDs, external retail barcodes, referral codes and payment references in separate fields.

5. Category, customer, advertiser and referral IDs

Every platform category and subcategoryâ€”including all planned catalogue sectorsâ€”must have a stable internal category ID. Each registered customer, seller, advertiser, artist applicant and business partner must receive a separate customer/account ID after registration is connected to the backend.

Each advertising campaign, enquiry, referral/lead and operation must receive its own reference linked to the correct account and category. Do not reuse one code for different people or operations, and do not put private personal information inside a QR/barcode.

For eligible products and services, buyers should be able to request a negotiated price or quote. The seller/partner can respond with an offer, quantity, currency, delivery terms and expiry date; both sides must confirm the agreed terms before an order is treated as accepted.

Mobile Money, bank cards and international payment options

The future payment plan may evaluate Mobile Money, bank transfer, bank cards, PayPal and other suitable providersâ€”including regional wallets where legally and technically supported. Availability, currencies, fees, identity checks and payout support must be confirmed for each target country and provider before activation. No payment option is represented as active today.

  • Use a reputable payment provider and its documented checkout/API flow.
  • Keep test/sandbox transactions separate from real-money transactions.
  • Never collect or store customers' banking passwords or sensitive authentication codes.

  • Only issue a final receipt or confirm a commission after server-side payment verification.
